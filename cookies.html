Cookie policy

Which cookies and similar technologies tobie.io uses, and how to change your choice.

Last updated 11 September 2026

In short

  • tobie.io sets no cookies.
  • It stores one item in your browser, tobie_consent, which remembers your privacy choice. This is strictly necessary and is never sent to us.
  • There are no analytics, advertising, social-media or other third-party cookies.
  • If we ever want to add optional technologies, we will update this policy and ask you first.

01What this policy covers

This policy covers cookies and any similar technology that stores or reads information on your device, such as your browser’s local storage. It applies to the website at tobie.io, operated by ZERO.BIM s.r.o. (see our legal notice).

04Third parties

The website does not embed third-party content such as videos, maps or social-media widgets. It does not load fonts or scripts from third-party services. The typefaces and the three.js graphics library are served from tobie.io itself. No third party sets cookies through this website.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting service processes technical request data, such as your IP address, at server level. This is not a cookie and does not depend on your choice in the privacy panel. It is explained in our privacy notice.

05Legal basis

Storing or reading information on your device is governed by Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC), implemented in the Czech Republic by § 89(3) of Act No. 127/2005 Coll., on Electronic Communications. Storage that is strictly necessary to provide a service you asked for, such as remembering your privacy choice, does not require consent. Any other storage requires your prior, informed and freely given consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
